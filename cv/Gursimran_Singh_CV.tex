\section{Research Summary}
Ph.D. researcher in \textbf{programmable network systems and security}. I design and evaluate line-rate security
functions (deep packet inspection, firewalls, intrusion detection) in \textbf{P4 on Intel Tofino} switches, characterize
the hardware limits that constrain them, and build tools that measure \textbf{Internet censorship and resilience}.
Two first-author publications; committee service for EuroSys, PPoPP, ICS, CoNEXT, and ACM~S3; adjunct teaching; industry
work on intent-based networking.\\[2pt]
{\small\textbf{Interests:} programmable data planes (P4, Tofino, SmartNICs/FPGAs) \,\textperiodcentered\, in-network security and collective computation
\,\textperiodcentered\, Internet measurement, censorship, and resilience under disaster \,\textperiodcentered\, security and privacy \,\textperiodcentered\, dependable systems under hardware limits.}

        {Oklahoma State University (2026 -- present) \,\textperiodcentered\, Rochester Institute of Technology (2021 -- 2025)}{}
  \vspace{1pt}
  \item \textbf{Programmable data-plane security on Intel Tofino} \hfill {\small 2021 -- Present}
  \begin{bullets}
    \item Design and implement application-layer security functions (deep packet inspection and pattern matching,
          stateful filtering, NIDS-style detection) in P4 on Intel Tofino and Tofino2 switches (Tofino Native
          Architecture), engineering around per-stage memory, restricted instruction sets, and parser limits.
    \item Develop packet-analysis and filtering algorithms that use recirculation, cross-packet flow state, and precomputed
          lookups to approach firewall and intrusion-detection capability at line rate; prototype and evaluate on Tofino
          hardware (P4 Studio SDE, BF-RT control plane).
    \item First-authored a systematization of programmable-data-plane security research (NeTCoM 2025, [P1]).
    \item \emph{Active directions:} correctness of inspection under recirculation and flow-state pressure (inspected versus merely
          forwarded throughput); safe evolution of security state across heterogeneous data paths (ASIC, SmartNIC/DPU, FPGA, host)
          with exactly-once enforcement and recovery; minimum equivalent firewall policies with checkable optimality certificates;
          orchestration and failure handling for in-network computing.
  \end{bullets}
  \item \textbf{Internet censorship and resilience measurement} \hfill {\small 2022 -- Present}
  \begin{bullets}
    \item Designed and implemented measurement tooling for the Russia--Ukraine war study: a classifier that
          detects \emph{soft} failures of website access (beyond outright connection failures) and a multi-protocol
          traceroute implementation that localizes where interference occurs along a path.
    \item Collected and analyzed longitudinal measurements from Ukraine, Russia, and the United States (March--July 2022),
          attributing reachability changes to network conditions versus filtering (ACM ASIA CCS 2023, [P2]).
    \item \emph{Active directions:} sustainability of refraction-networking deployments when operators withdraw, recovering access
          without losing resistance to blocking; probe-induced bias in censorship and resilience measurement.
  \end{bullets}
\end{entries}
